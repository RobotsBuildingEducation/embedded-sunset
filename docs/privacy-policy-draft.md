# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 8, 2026**

This policy covers **Piyali** and **Robots Building Education**, including piyali.app, nosabos.app, robotsbuildingeducation.com, and embedded-sunset.app. In this policy, “we” means the operator of these services.

For privacy questions or requests, email **sheilfer@robotsbuildingeducation.com**.

## Information we use

We use the information needed to run the apps and save your work. Depending on the features you use, this includes:

- Your Nostr public account key, display name, public profile information, language preferences, and account settings.
- Learning progress, exercise answers, assessment results, feedback, goals, streaks, achievements, and activity dates.
- Messages, conversation transcripts, submitted code, project ideas, saved builds, stories, charts, and other content you enter or save.
- Microphone audio and recognized speech when you use voice features, and recordings you choose to save.
- Patreon membership identifiers, subscription status, entitlement information, and authorization tokens when you connect Patreon.
- Wallet and payment information needed for supported Cashu, Lightning, or Nostr transactions.
- Technical information such as IP addresses, browser and device information, usage events, cookies, and diagnostic or security logs.

Not every feature collects every category. For example, Piyali saves language-learning and citizenship-study records, while Robots Building Education also supports coding projects, charts, and chess. The investment calculator currently saves its scenario inputs in your browser.

A Nostr public key is a persistent identifier. Using a nickname does not make your account or activity anonymous, especially if you use the same key elsewhere.

## How we use information

We use information to provide learning activities, respond to your requests, generate feedback and speech, personalize practice, save and synchronize progress, verify membership access, operate community and payment features, answer support messages, understand app usage, fix problems, and prevent abuse.

Learning features may use your recent answers, mistakes, or conversation context to suggest future practice. AI-generated scores, explanations, and recommendations can be inaccurate.

## AI, speech, and service providers

The apps use **Google / Firebase / Google Cloud**, **OpenAI**, **Cloudflare**, **Patreon**, and independent **Nostr relays**, depending on the feature. Wallet features also communicate with Cashu mints and other supported payment services. Robots Building Education includes Coinbase / OnchainKit integrations. Externally hosted media and browser speech features may communicate with their own providers.

When you use AI features, your messages, submitted work, relevant learning context, or microphone audio may be sent to Google or OpenAI to generate a response, translation, transcription, assessment, or spoken output. Browser speech recognition may also process audio through a service supplied by your browser provider.

Providers may retain information for operating their services, security, abuse prevention, or feature storage under their applicable terms and account settings. Processing a request does not mean the request is immediately deleted. Details are available in [OpenAI’s data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data), [Google Cloud’s AI data information](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/vertex-ai-zero-data-retention), and [Firebase’s privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

Avoid entering passwords, private keys, government identifiers, or sensitive information that is unnecessary for your learning activity.

## Public activity on Nostr

**Some app activity is public.** Nostr is a network of independently operated servers. Published information can be viewed, copied, indexed, and redistributed by other people and applications.

Depending on how you create your account, the app may publish a profile and introductory post. Achievement synchronization may publish earned achievements and timestamps. Team features may publish team names, member public keys, membership changes, and learning progress. Progress can include your display name, language, scores, completed work, goals, streaks, experience points, and companion information.

Some publication happens automatically. Piyali can publish team progress while its Teams view is open. Robots Building Education can publish course progress when completed work is saved. Automatic learning posts can also share activity or grades when their posting setting is enabled. A team creator may publish your public key when adding you to a team.

**The automatic-posts setting does not control every public-sharing feature.** Achievements and team or course-progress events use separate publishing paths. Using the same Nostr identity across the apps allows public activity to be linked across them.

Leaving a team, replacing an event, or removing something from an app does not guarantee removal of copies held by independent relays or other people.

## Accounts, browser storage, and audio

The apps use cookies and browser storage to remember account access, preferences, saved work, sessions, offline data, and cached media. If you create or enter a private Nostr key in an app, it may be stored in your browser. A compatible signing extension can instead handle signing through its own controls.

Keep your private key secure. Clearing browser storage can remove locally saved keys and work. **Signing out or clearing browser storage does not delete cloud records or public Nostr events.**

Piyali’s saved **Voice Journey recordings** are encrypted on your device before upload. Playback requires the matching key or compatible signer. Some metadata, such as account identifiers, language, milestones, and timestamps, is stored separately. Available recording controls let you delete saved recordings. This protection does not apply to every chat, learning record, or live voice session.

Generated text-to-speech audio may be cached in your browser and on shared Cloudflare servers. Cached audio can be accessible through its URL, and the URL may contain the text being spoken or voice-persona settings.

## Memberships, payments, and analytics

Connecting Patreon links your membership to your learning account. The app stores encrypted authorization tokens and membership details to verify access. Disconnecting that link does not cancel your Patreon subscription or delete your learning records. Subscription cancellation is handled through Patreon.

Payment features communicate with the relevant wallet services, mints, and relays. Some wallet content is encrypted, while payment events and related metadata may be public. Payment activity should not be assumed to be anonymous.

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage and Firebase App Check with reCAPTCHA to help prevent abuse. These services may process cookies, identifiers, device information, and interaction data. Infrastructure providers also process technical requests and logs.

## Retention, requests, and your choices

Learning records and saved content may remain in our cloud systems so you can return to your work. Other information may remain in membership records, analytics, operational logs, caches, or provider systems. Retention varies by the information, feature, and provider; we do not promise that all information expires after a fixed number of days.

You can decline or revoke microphone permission, change available posting settings, turn off supported notifications, and use available content-deletion controls.

To ask about accessing, correcting, or deleting information, email **sheilfer@robotsbuildingeducation.com** with the relevant app and your public account key. We may need to verify that the request concerns your account. **Do not email your private key.** Rights and exceptions depend on applicable law. Some records may need to be retained for security, payment, or legal reasons, and we cannot guarantee deletion of copies held by independent public relays or recipients.

## Security, international processing, and children

We use protections such as HTTPS, application verification, and encryption for specific kinds of information. Ordinary learning records and chats are not all encrypted with a key available only to you, and providers process the information sent to them. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate.

Parents or guardians with questions about a child’s information, or who want us to review an access or deletion request concerning a child, can contact **sheilfer@robotsbuildingeducation.com**. School users should also follow their school’s rules for using external services.
